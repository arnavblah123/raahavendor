# How to use the Raaha Vendor Tracker

A short guide for everyone at Raaha. Keep the app open on your phone.

---

## Start here every morning

Open the app. The **Today** screen is the only thing you need to look at.

It shows you, top to bottom:

### 🔴 Overdue
Orders that are **past the date the vendor promised** and still have not been
sent. The big red number is how many days late they are. The oldest one is at
the top.

**These are the ones to call first.**

### 🟠 Due today
Reminders that fall today, and orders that are supposed to be dispatched today.

### 🟡 Missed follow-ups
Reminders that came up on an earlier day and were never logged. They stay here
until somebody deals with them — they will not quietly disappear.

### 🔵 Coming up
The next three days. Tap the heading to open it. Nothing to do here yet.

At the top of the screen there is also a row of numbers: how many orders are
open, how many pieces are still pending, how many went out this month, and the
average delay.

---

## Following up with a vendor

Each card has three buttons.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📞 **Call** | Dials the vendor. |
| 💬 **WhatsApp** | Opens WhatsApp with the whole message already written — vendor's name, order number, what was ordered, the promised date and a polite ask. Read it, then press send. |
| ✅ **Log** | Records what happened. **Always do this.** |

**Logging is the important one.** If you do not log it, the app has no way to
know you called, the reminder stays on the list, and the same vendor gets chased
twice tomorrow.

### What to fill in when you log

- **Contacted via** — call, WhatsApp, visit or email.
- **Spoke to** — the person's name, if you got one.
- **What did they say?** — write it in your own words. *"Embroidery finished,
  stitching will take 10 more days"* is perfect.
- **New promised date** — **only fill this in if the date has actually
  changed.** If they said 10 more days, put the new date here.
- **Next action** — anything to remember, e.g. *"Ask for photos on Monday"*.
- Tick **Goods are ready** if they told you it is ready to send.

Press **Save**. The card disappears from your list.

### If you cannot reach them

Press **Snooze 2 days** at the bottom of the card. The reminder comes back in
two days instead of nagging you today. The app keeps count of how many times
something has been snoozed.

## What happens when a vendor asks for more time

This is normal and the app is built for it.

When you enter a **new promised date**, the app:

- **keeps the original date they promised** — so nobody can quietly hide a delay,
- **reschedules the remaining reminders** across the days that are actually left,
- adds a red **Revised 2×** label on the order.

That red label is the point. A vendor who keeps pushing the date is the real
problem, and now you can see it at a glance — and so can Archana, on their
vendor page.

## Placing a new order

Tap **Add** at the bottom of the screen.

1. **Choose the vendor.** If they are new, tap **New** and add them there and
   then — you do not have to leave the screen.
2. **Order date** — usually today. If you are entering an older order, just pick
   the real date; the app will handle it and mark any reminders that have
   already passed as skipped.
3. **Lead time** — how many days the vendor said they need. The expected
   dispatch date is worked out for you.
4. **Follow-up schedule** — leave it on *Standard* unless you have a reason.
   *Tight* chases more often; *Light* chases twice.
5. You will see a gold box: **"You will be reminded on…"** with the actual dates.
   Check it looks sensible.
6. **Items** — one row per design. Put the design code in if you have it, it
   makes searching much easier later. **Count in pieces or sets** — this is for
   finished garments, never for fabric by the metre.
7. **For every piece, add a photo.** Tap *Add a photo of the piece* — it opens
   the camera, or you can pick one from the gallery. Photograph the design,
   the sample or the reference picture. This photo goes on the purchase order
   and is what the goods are checked against when they arrive.
8. **If the vendor is making it to size, tick *This piece is made to
   measurements*** and enter them. Tap the grey chips (Bust, Waist, Length…)
   to add the usual ones, or type your own. Choose inches or cm at the top.
   Leave the box unticked for standard sizes.
9. Tap **Place order**. If any piece has no photo, or measurements are ticked
   but empty, the app stops and lists them — go back and add them, or place
   the order anyway if that is deliberate.

### Raising the purchase order

The moment the order is placed you are asked **"Raise a purchase order for the
vendor now?"** Tap **Create purchase order**. That is all — the PO number
(`PO/2026-27/0012`) is generated for you and can never repeat.

The PO opens as a document showing every piece with its photo and measurements.
From there:

- **Print / save as PDF** — on a phone, choose *Save as PDF* in the print
  screen and you have a file to share.
- **Send on WhatsApp** — opens WhatsApp with a message listing the pieces and
  the PO number. Attach the PDF if the vendor wants the full document.

If you tap *Not now*, you can raise the PO later from the order page. There is
one PO per order.

## When the goods arrive

Open the order and tap the big **Dispatched** button.

You will see every item with **Ordered · Already sent · Balance**, and a box
pre-filled with the balance.

**Change the numbers to what actually turned up.** If a design did not come at
all, set it to 0.

Then fill in the dispatch date, the transporter and docket number if you have
them, and press save.

### If only part of the order came

That is very common and completely fine.

The app will tell you how many pieces are still pending and ask you **when the
vendor has promised the balance**. Enter that date.

The order then stays open, keeps showing on your morning list with a
**Partially dispatched (12/20 pcs)** label, and you get a fresh short set of
reminders for the balance. Nothing gets forgotten.

When the last piece arrives, record that dispatch too and the order closes
itself automatically.

### After it arrives at the shop — inwarding

Open the order and tap **Inward goods**. (It only appears once the order has a
purchase order — raise one first if you skipped it.)

For every piece on the PO:

1. **Received now** — how many actually came. It is pre-filled with everything
   still expected; set it to 0 if that design did not come.
2. **Price per piece** — copy it from the vendor's invoice exactly. You will
   not see this again afterwards; only Archana can.
3. **Check the measurements** — if the order had measurements, you will see
   them listed with a box next to each. **Measure the piece and type what you
   get.** The difference shows up straight away, in red if it is not the same.
4. **If anything differs, the app tells you to flag it.** Tick *Flag a problem
   for the owner* and **write down what is wrong** in plain words — *"Waist
   came 31, ordered 30 — too tight"*. You cannot save until you have written
   it. You can flag a piece for any other reason too: damage, wrong colour,
   wrong fabric.

Then fill in the date, the invoice number and your name, and press **Save
inward**. If pieces are still to come, it asks when the vendor has promised
them, just like a partial dispatch.

When the last piece is in, the order moves to **Received** on its own. Once you
are completely done with it, set the stage to **Closed** and it moves out of
the active lists.

### What happens to flagged pieces

They appear at the top of the **Today** screen and on the **Inwards** page
until Archana has looked at them and tapped **Mark as seen**, with a note on
what was decided — accepted at a discount, sent back for alteration, replaced.
That note then shows on the order for everyone.

## Finding an old order

Tap **Orders**. You can search by order number, vendor, product name or design
code. Use the filter button for a particular vendor, date range, or to show only
overdue or only urgent orders.

**Export CSV** downloads whatever you are currently looking at as a spreadsheet.

---

## Vendor pages

Tap **Vendors**, then any vendor. You will see every order you have placed with
them and a **grade**:

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Reliable — average delay of 2 days or less |
| **B** | Usually fine — up to a week late |
| **C** | Needs chasing — up to a fortnight late |
| **D** | Chronic — more than a fortnight late |

There is also **Sent in one go**, which shows how often they deliver the whole
order at once instead of splitting it. A vendor who sends half on time and drags
the rest out for three weeks will look fine on delay alone — this is what
catches them, and the app puts a warning on their page when it spots that
pattern.

This page is what to open before renegotiating with a vendor.

---

## A few rules that keep this working

1. **Log every call.** Even a two-second "still not ready" one. An unlogged
   follow-up looks identical to no follow-up at all.
2. **Only enter a new promised date when the date genuinely changed.** Otherwise
   the delay figures stop meaning anything.
3. **Never delete an order.** If it is not happening, set the stage to
   **Cancelled**. The history is worth keeping.
4. **Count pieces honestly at dispatch.** Every balance number in the app comes
   from what you type there.

## Common questions

**Does the app send WhatsApp messages by itself?**
No. It writes the message for you and opens WhatsApp — you always read it and
press send yourself.

**Will I lose reminders if I do not open the app for a few days?**
No. Nothing is pushed or expires. Everything you missed will be waiting under
**Missed follow-ups**.

**I cannot see any prices or amounts.**
That is intentional for staff accounts. Only Archana can see money. That
includes the invoice price you typed at inward — you can enter it, not read it.

**The photo will not upload.**
Check you are signed in and have a signal; photos are shrunk on your phone and
sent straight to storage. If it says storage is not set up, Archana needs to run
migration 0002 in Supabase.

**The measurements were fine but I still need to flag something.**
Tick *Flag a problem for the owner* on that piece and write it down. A flag
does not need a measurement difference — damage, colour and fabric problems
count just as much.

**I entered something wrong.**
Open the order and fix it. If you need to change the original promised date or
delete something, ask Archana — only an admin can do those.

**The vendor's number does not work for WhatsApp.**
Open the vendor's page and check the phone number. Indian mobile numbers work
with or without +91.
